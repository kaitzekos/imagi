# Lesson plan, Tomer's flow: can your classmates escape a world you invented?

Draft for Tomer's review. Not tried with children. Timings are targets: nothing has been timed with a class, and the Lovable build wait for eight pairs at once is not measured.

This is the format Tomer asked for: what I say, what the children do and click, timings, partner turns, where they make their own decisions, how I help when someone gets stuck, setup, saving and reopening, sharing, and which parts happen where. Paper first, then Lovable inside imagi Edu. The Escape Builder prototype is not used by the children in this version.

| | |
|---|---|
| Learners | 16 Grade 7 learners in 8 pairs (pair 1 = learners 1 and 2, pair 2 = learners 3 and 4, and so on) |
| Devices | 8 shared laptops with Chrome and Wi-Fi, one projector laptop |
| Time | 45 minutes, plus an optional 15-minute extension |
| Adults | One facilitator |
| Where it happens | Paper, then Lovable inside imagi Edu. The demo game runs on the projector only |
| Demo game | Alien Snack Shop: <https://imagi-mockup.vercel.app/alien-snack-shop-v1.html> |

About the demo: Alien Snack Shop is a hand-built example of what the starting prompt asks Lovable to make. Kai wrote it by hand; it is not a Lovable build. It sits on a site outside imagi, so it is shown on the projector only. Its code is 742: the receipt on the counter lists Star 4, Moon 2, Rocket 7, and the lock shows Rocket, Star, Moon.

## Minute by minute

| Min | Where and keyboard | What I say | Children do and click | Children decide | If someone is stuck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | [Projector]. Teacher, then a volunteer | Open Alien Snack Shop. "Can your classmates escape a world you invented? Today you'll build one, test it on a classmate and make it better. Someone built this one by hand, to show the kind of game you'll ask the AI to make. Who wants to try it?" | The volunteer (by learner number) clicks things in the shop, tries a wrong code, presses Hint, finds the receipt and the lock pictures, types the code and reaches the snack party. The class calls out guesses | The volunteer: whether to press Hint or try again | "Want a hint, or one more try?" Never say the code |
| 2-5 | [Paper]. Nobody | Hand out the starting check. Odd numbers get A, even numbers get B. "On your own. Write what you think. There's no trick." | Starting check alone, up to 3 minutes. No partner, no AI | Their own answers | Read a question aloud if asked, and note "read aloud" on the sheet. Keep the first answer before any coaching |
| 5-8 | [Paper]. Nobody | "Your idea first. The AI comes after. Each of you writes at least one line. Don't copy the snack shop: this is your world." | Fill the idea card: world, clue, code, ending, colours and mood. A phrase or a sketch is enough | Their world, clue, code, ending and look, before any AI suggestion | "Who will play it? What would make them smile?" or "What is one thing in your room that could hide a number?" Wait. Write their words, not yours |
| 8-10 (target, untimed. reference/assignment.md asks for a first playable game by minute 10. With the prompt sent at 8-10 and up to 3 minutes of build, it may arrive at 11-13. Loom 1 times one build on 6 Nov (result in checkpoint/test-log.md); it still needs timing with children) | [imagi/Lovable]. Partner A | "Copy the prompt, fill the five blanks from your card, and send it. While it builds, each of you writes one prediction." | Partner A: in imagi Edu, open Lovable; start a new project named "Escape - pair N"; paste the starting prompt; fill the five blanks from the card in their own words; send. Both, on the playtest card: "When I type a wrong code, I think the game will ___." | The words in the five blanks; their prediction | Can't log in after 3 minutes: paper backup. Build over 3 minutes: refresh once, then paper backup |
| 10-17 | [imagi/Lovable]. Partner A | "Was your prediction right? What would make it feel more like your world?" | Play Version 1: click things, a wrong code, an empty code, Hint, the right code, Restart. Then send one change in their own words: the clue, a message or the ending. Play it again | Which part to make more their own, in their words | "Lovable says it's done. What happened when you tried it?" No playable game by minute 12: paper backup |
| 17-23 | [imagi/Lovable]. The player | Pairs 1 and 2 swap, 3 and 4, 5 and 6, 7 and 8. "Players, play. Creators, no helping and no pointing. Watch where they get stuck." At minute 20: "Swap. The other two now." | 17-20: one learner plays the neighbours' game on their laptop while one of its creators watches. 20-23: the other two swap in. Watchers write on the playtest card: "The player clicked ___ first. They got stuck at ___." | Players: when to use Hint. Creators: what to write down | Player stuck: "Want a hint, or one more try?" Creator wants to help: "Watch where they get stuck. That's what you'll fix." |
| 23-31 | [Laptop + paper]. Partner B | "What did your player do? Which change helps them? Why that one?" | Each child proposes one change. The pair picks one and writes on the playtest card: "Version 1 works when ___." Then Change / Because / Keep / Check. Partner B types it into Lovable and sends. Both predict: "After this, a new player will ___." | Which thing the player did matters most; which change answers it; what must stay | Pair disagrees: "What does each of you want the player to feel? Which change tests that?" Vague line: show one row from the vague-and-clear table in the Change / Because / Keep / Check guide, once |
| 31-35 | [imagi/Lovable]. Partner B | "Test the change, then one thing that should still work." Then: "Lovable says it's done. What happened when you tried it?" | Test the change. Then test a kept rule: a wrong code stays shut, and Restart clears everything. Write the result next to the prediction. If there's time, a learner from another pair who hasn't seen the game plays it | Whether the retest passed, and what to tell Lovable if it didn't | Change broke the game: restore is not tested yet. Send: "Undo the last change only. The game should work like before: ___. Keep everything else." |
| 35-38 | [Paper]. Nobody | Hand out the other check. "Same as before: on your own." | The other check, alone (A if they did B first, B if they did A) | Their own answers | Keep the first answer before any coaching |
| 38-45 | [Laptop + paper]. Both | "Close your Lovable tab. Open your project again from imagi and check it's still there." Then: "What would you add next?" If a minute is left, ask two pairs: "What did you change that made it work better?" | Close the Lovable tab. Reopen the project from imagi and check Version 2 is there. Fill the maker note. Fill the take-home card: the game, one choice we made, the next idea | Their next idea, and their first step toward it | Project missing after reopening: note the pair number and tell Liba. Saving on the student route is not tested yet |

Extension (15 minutes, optional): another upgrade from the challenge ladder, or a second test with a new player. Never for finishing the core.

## Partner turns

| Block | Partner A (odd number) | Partner B (even number) |
|---|---|---|
| Idea card (5-8) | Writes at least one line | Writes at least one line |
| Send and play Version 1 (8-17) | At the keyboard | Writes a prediction; suggests what to make more their own |
| Playtest (17-23) | Plays once, watches once | Plays once, watches once |
| Version 2 (23-35) | Proposes one change; says what to test and what should happen | At the keyboard; proposes one change |
| Save and note (38-45) | Own maker note and take-home card | Own maker note and take-home card |

A child may say or sketch an idea if writing is hard. The partner or the teacher writes it down in the child's words.

## Where children make their own decisions

| Minute | The decision | What the teacher does |
|---|---|---|
| 5-8 | Their world, clue, code, ending, colours and mood | Asks one question and waits. Never fills the blank |
| 8-10 | The words in the five blanks; their prediction | Checks the words came from the card |
| 10-17 | Which part to make more their own | Asks what would make it feel like their world |
| 17-23 | Players: when to press Hint. Creators: what to write down | Offers "Want a hint, or one more try?" |
| 23-31 | Which thing the player did matters most; which change answers it; what to keep | Asks what each choice would do to the player |
| 31-35 | Whether the retest passed | Asks what happened, not whether Lovable said "Done" |
| 38-45 | Their next idea and first step | Writes nothing for them |

## When someone is stuck

| Problem | Do or say |
|---|---|
| Can't think of an idea | Ask one at a time: "Who will play it? What would make them smile?" "What is one thing in your room that could hide a number?" Wait. Write their words, not yours |
| Wants to copy the snack shop | "That one's taken. What's a place you'd like to be locked in?" |
| Two clue ideas, can't choose | "Name two places the clue could be. Which is fairer for a first-time player?" |
| Unsuitable idea | "Would a 12-year-old in another class enjoy this? What could you change so they would?" |
| Can't log in | [Steps from Liba]. Over 3 minutes: paper backup |
| Build takes over 3 minutes | Refresh once. Then paper backup |
| No game by minute 12 | Paper backup. Note it on the per-pair sheet |
| Lovable added sign-in, a database or pictures from the internet | Send: "Remove the ___. Keep the room, clue, lock, hint, ending and Restart." |
| Lovable says "Done" | "What happened when you tried it?" Then run the Check line |
| A change broke the game | Restore is not tested yet. Send: "Undo the last change only. The game should work like before: ___. Keep everything else." If that fails, paper backup |
| Player stuck | "Want a hint, or one more try?" Never give the code |
| Creator wants to help the player | "Watch where they get stuck. That's what you'll fix." |
| Pair argues about the change | "What does each of you want the player to feel? Which change tests that?" |
| "Make it easier" | "What did your player do just before they got stuck?" |
| Hint gives the code | "Should the hint give the answer or help the player find it? What would each choice do to the puzzle?" |
| Project missing after reopening | Note the pair number; tell Liba |

Notice when a player asks to make their own game. Polite praise doesn't count.

## Sharing

| Where | How | Who |
|---|---|---|
| In class | A classmate plays on the creator's laptop. Nothing leaves the room | Learners |
| Take-home | The take-home card with a drawing or a screenshot of the game. No link | Learners, with the teacher |
| A link to play at home | Only if Liba has agreed. An adult publishes the game from Lovable. After that, the same imagi link opens for anyone. The adult opens it in a private browser window to check it opens without sign-in and shows only the game. The adult decides who receives it. An unlisted link is not private | Adult only. Play outside the class is recorded as untested |

Before publishing, the link inside imagi Edu asks for sign-in. After the teacher publishes from Lovable, the same link opens for anyone, so publishing is the adult's sharing decision. Children never publish, and no link goes on a take-home card unless Liba has agreed.

## Evidence

| What | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Starting check (A or B) | 2-5 | Alone, up to 3 minutes. Odd numbers A, even numbers B |
| End check (the other one) | 35-38 | Alone, up to 3 minutes |
| Idea card, playtest card, maker note | During class | Collected at the end |
| Observation sheet and per-pair sheet | During class | [scoring-guide.md](scoring-guide.md) |

Score each check on reason, instruction and test, 0 to 2 each, with [scoring-guide.md](scoring-guide.md). Score each child alone. A pair's game is not an individual score. These are classroom checks, not validated measures.

| Skill | Where you see it | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| Making and explaining choices | Idea card; the "Because" line; maker note line 1; the pair's talk at 23-31 | The idea is theirs, in their words. They can say why they picked one change over another |
| Giving AI clear instructions | The prompt in Lovable's chat; Change / Because / Keep / Check on the card; check Q2 | Says what changes, for whom, and what must stay |
| Judging the AI's output | Prediction next to the result; the answer to "Lovable says it's done. What happened?" | They play it before they believe it. They describe what they saw, not a new wish |
| Testing and improving | Playtest line; retest result; check Q1 and Q3 | The change comes from what the player did. They test the change and a kept rule |

Per pair, record: first playable minute, paper backup used, adult rescues, build waits, the change chosen and why, and whether a player asked to make their own game.
