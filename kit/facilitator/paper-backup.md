# Paper backup

Draft v0. For a pair with no working game by minute 12. Children still choose, play, change and retest. Record paper use separately from AI builds.

## Print (one set per pair)

1. Room card: a drawn snack shop with a counter, a receipt, three aliens, a shelf and a locked fridge. Leave space to draw.
2. Close-up cards, one per object, face down: the receipt says "Star = 4, Moon = 2, Rocket = 7". The fridge lock card shows Rocket, Star, Moon and three empty boxes.
3. Hint card: "Look at what the aliens bought."
4. Wrong-code card: "The fridge burps. Still locked."
5. Ending card: "Snack party!"
6. Blank set of the same cards for the pair's own world.

## How to play

1. The pair makes their own room on the blank cards: world, clue, code, ending.
2. The pair is the game. A player points at things; the creators flip the close-up card.
3. Wrong code: the creators read the wrong-code card. Hint: they hand over the hint card.
4. The creators watch and write one thing the player did.
5. Version 2: they change one card, say why, and test with a new player.

## Answer key (facilitator only)

Alien Snack Shop: 742. Rocket = 7, Star = 4, Moon = 2.
