# Class script: one pair's journey, for a Loom

Draft for Tomer's review. A roleplay, not a recording of a real class. Nothing here has been tried with children.

Kai plays the Teacher and Learner 7, and reads the short lines for the others. The game is built live in Lovable inside imagi Edu on Kai's account, which is an educator account, not the student route. The prototype is not used.

The demo game is Alien Snack Shop (<https://imagi-mockup.vercel.app/alien-snack-shop-v1.html>). It is a hand-built example of what the starting prompt asks Lovable to make. Kai wrote it by hand; it is not a Lovable build. Its code is 742.

## Cast

| Part | Who | In the lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher | Kai | Runs the class from the run sheet and lesson plan |
| Learner 7 | Kai | Pair 4, Partner A: types Version 1 |
| Learner 8 | Kai, brief lines | Pair 4, Partner B: types Version 2 |
| Learner 5 | Kai, brief lines | Pair 3, the neighbouring pair: plays pair 4's game at minute 17 |
| Learner 3 | Kai, brief lines | Pair 2: a player who hasn't seen the game, for the retest |

## Before you record

- Close other tabs. Hide anything that shows an email address or password.
- Say once that the imagi account shows Tomer's name.
- Open Alien Snack Shop and click Restart so it starts fresh.
- Have the idea card and playtest card on paper, or as a blank page on screen.
- The player's moves below are scripted for the roleplay. The game's answers are whatever Lovable built. Read out what actually appears. If the game does something different from the "Expected" line, say so and keep it in.
- Cut the build waits in the edit, and say how long each one took.
- Lines marked "if" or "say what you see" depend on what the game really does. Change them to match.

## The example idea

This is an example, made up for the roleplay. It is not a real learner's idea, and it is not a model to give children. In class, each pair writes their own.

| Idea card line | Example, as Learners 7 and 8 might say it |
|---|---|
| My world | (Learner 7) "A pet shop at night. The hamsters locked everybody in." |
| My clue | (Learner 8) "The fish tank. There's 3 red fish, 1 blue fish and 5 yellow fish. A note on the tank says: Count the fish: red, then blue, then yellow." |
| My code | (Learner 8) "315." |
| My ending | (Learner 7) "The hamsters open the door and do a little dance." |
| My colours and mood | (Learner 7) "Green and orange. Silly, not scary." |

## The script

Class minutes are on the left. Suggested Loom length is about 14 minutes plus the live waits you cut.

### Class minute 0-2: the demo [Projector] (Loom: about 1 minute)

[Opens Alien Snack Shop on the projector laptop.]

**Teacher:** "Can your classmates escape a world you invented? Today you'll build one, test it on a classmate and make it better. Someone built this one by hand, to show the kind of game you'll ask the AI to make. Who wants to try it first? Learner 7."

**Teacher (to camera):** "I made this one by hand. It isn't a Lovable build. It shows what the starting prompt asks Lovable to make."

[Learner 7 clicks an alien and the shelf, and reads each message aloud.]

**Learner 7:** "Um, 111?"

[Types 111. Clicks Open. Expected: the fridge stays shut and a wrong-code message appears. The code is not shown. Read out the message.]

**Teacher:** "Want a hint, or one more try?"

**Learner 7:** "Hint."

[Clicks Hint. Reads the hint aloud. Clicks the receipt on the counter: Star 4, Moon 2, Rocket 7. Clicks the fridge: the lock shows Rocket, Star, Moon. Types 742. Clicks Open. Expected: the fridge opens and the snack party shows.]

**Teacher:** "You escaped. What helped you most?"

**Learner 7:** "The hint told me where to look. Then I had to put the numbers in the lock's order."

### Class minute 2-5: the starting check [Paper] (Loom: about 30 seconds)

[Shows check A and check B on screen.]

**Teacher:** "On your own. Write what you think. There's no trick. Odd numbers, you have A. Even numbers, B."

**Teacher (to camera):** "Learner 7 is odd, so A now and B at the end. Learner 8 does B now. Laptops stay closed. If someone asks me to read a question aloud, I do, and I write 'read aloud' on their sheet. I keep their first answer."

### Class minute 5-8: the idea card [Paper] (Loom: about 1.5 minutes)

**Teacher:** "Your idea first. The AI comes after. Each of you writes at least one line."

**Learner 7:** "A pet shop at night. The hamsters locked everybody in."

[Writes it on the idea card.]

**Learner 7:** "And when you escape, the hamsters open the door and do a little dance. Green and orange. Silly, not scary."

**Learner 8:** "I don't know what the clue is."

This is the stuck moment. The Teacher asks one question and waits. No suggestions.

**Teacher:** "What is one thing in your pet shop that could hide a number?"

[Pause.]

**Learner 8:** "...The fish tank? You could count the fish. 3 red, 1 blue, 5 yellow. And a note on the tank says: Count the fish: red, then blue, then yellow."

**Teacher:** "So the player has to count. Write it in your own words."

**Learner 8:** "So the code is 315."

**Teacher (to camera):** "I asked one question and waited. The fish tank is Learner 8's idea. If they'd stayed stuck, my next question would be 'Who will play it? What would make them smile?' I never fill the blank for them."

### Class minute 8-10: send the starting prompt [imagi/Lovable] (Loom: about 1.5 minutes, plus the build wait)

**Teacher:** "Copy the prompt, fill the five blanks from your card, and send it. While it builds, each of you writes one prediction."

[Learner 7 at the keyboard. Opens imagi Edu. Opens Lovable from imagi. Starts a new project and names it "Escape - pair 4".]

**Teacher (to camera):** "No names in the project, only the pair number. On the student route these clicks aren't tested yet. This is my educator account."

[Opens the shared starting-prompt document. Copies the whole box. Pastes it into Lovable's chat box. Fills the five blanks under "Our game" with the idea card words. Clicks send.]

**Teacher:** "While it builds: 'When I type a wrong code, I think the game will...'"

**Learner 7:** "...stay locked and say something funny."

**Learner 8:** "I think the door will shake."

[Both write their prediction on the playtest card. Note the time the build started.]

**Teacher (to camera):** "If a build is still going after 3 minutes, I refresh once. After that, or with no game by minute 12, the pair moves to the paper backup. This build took ___ minutes."

### Class minute 10-17: play Version 1, make it yours [imagi/Lovable] (Loom: about 2 minutes)

**Teacher:** "Was your prediction right? Play it."

[Learner 7 at the keyboard. Clicks two or three things in the room and reads each message aloud.]

[Clicks Open with the code box empty. Expected: "Nothing typed. It stays locked."]

**Learner 7:** "Empty doesn't work. Good."

[Types 111. Clicks Open. Expected: the door stays shut, the code box shakes and a wrong-code message shows. The code is not shown.]

**Learner 8 (if it shook):** "It shook! I was right."

**Learner 7 (if the message was funny):** "And it said something funny. I was right too."

If a prediction was wrong, the learner says so: "I thought ___, but it ___." That is a result too.

[Clicks Hint. Expected: the hint points to the fish tank and doesn't say 315. Clicks the fish tank. Expected: a close-up of the fish and the note "Count the fish: red, then blue, then yellow." Types 315. Expected: the door opens and the ending shows. Clicks Play again or Restart. Expected: the box is empty, the message is cleared and the door is locked.]

**Teacher (to camera):** "Lovable said 'Done' before we played. It only counts now, because we tried it ourselves."

**Teacher:** "What would make it feel more like your world?"

**Learner 7:** "The wrong-code message. I want it to say: 'The hamsters laugh at you. Still locked.'"

[Learner 7 types into Lovable, in their own words: "When the code is wrong, the message says: The hamsters laugh at you. Still locked." Sends. When it finishes, types 111 again. Expected: the new message shows and the door stays shut.]

### Class minute 17-23: someone else plays [imagi/Lovable] (Loom: about 2 minutes)

**Teacher:** "Pairs 3 and 4, swap. Players, play. Creators, no helping and no pointing. Watch where they get stuck."

**Teacher (to camera):** "Learner 5 from pair 3 plays pair 4's game on pair 4's laptop, while Learner 7 watches. At minute 20 the other two swap in, so every child plays once and watches once."

[As Learner 5: clicks the door first. Clicks Open with nothing typed. Types 123. Clicks Open. Clicks the door again.]

**Learner 5:** "I'm stuck."

**Teacher:** "Want a hint, or one more try?"

**Learner 5:** "Hint."

[Clicks Hint. Clicks the fish tank. Reads the note aloud, then counts in that order: red 3, blue 1, yellow 5. Types 315. Expected: the door opens and the ending shows.]

[Read out the ending as it appears.]

[Learner 7 writes on the playtest card: "The player clicked the door first. They got stuck at the door. They typed codes before they looked around, and found the fish tank only after Hint."]

**Teacher (to camera):** "I didn't give the code. I offered a choice. Learner 7 wrote what the player did, not what they should have done."

### Class minute 23-31: decide and change [Laptop + paper] (Loom: about 2.5 minutes, plus the build wait)

**Teacher:** "What did your player do? Which change helps them? Why that one?"

**Learner 7:** "Make the hint just tell you 315."

**Learner 8:** "Or make the fish tank wiggle when you get it wrong."

**Teacher:** "Should the hint give the answer or help them find it? What would each choice do to the puzzle?"

**Learner 8:** "If it says 315 there's no puzzle. The wiggle makes them look, but they still have to count."

**Learner 7:** "OK. The wiggle. Because our player kept typing at the door."

**Teacher (to camera):** "The pair chose. I asked one question about what each choice does to the player."

[Learner 8 takes the keyboard. The pair writes on the playtest card first.]

**Learner 8 (writing):** "Version 1 works when 315 opens the door."

[Learner 8 types into Lovable and sends:]

```text
Change: after a wrong code, make the fish tank wiggle and the fish blow bubbles for 2 seconds, so the player looks at it.
Because: our player kept typing codes at the door and didn't look around.
Keep: the code 315, the hint, the wrong-code message, the ending and Restart.
Check: we will type 111 and expect the tank to wiggle and the door to stay shut. Then Restart, and expect the wiggle to stop and the code box to be empty.
```

**Learner 7:** "After this, a new player will look at the fish tank after one wrong code."

[Writes the prediction on the card. Note the build time.]

### Class minute 31-35: retest [imagi/Lovable] (Loom: about 1.5 minutes)

**Teacher:** "Lovable says it's done. What happened when you tried it? Test the change, then one thing that should still work."

[Learner 8 at the keyboard. Types 111. Clicks Open. Expected: the fish tank wiggles, bubbles show, the door stays shut, the wrong-code message shows and the code is not shown.]

**Learner 8 (say what you see):** "It wiggles. Door's still shut."

[Clicks Restart. Expected: no wiggle, code box empty, door locked.]

[Types 315. Clicks Open. Expected: the door opens and the ending shows.]

**Learner 7:** "And 315 still works. That's our keep."

[Writes the result next to the prediction. If anything didn't match, say exactly what you saw and type it into Lovable as: "I typed ___ and ___ happened. It should ___. Keep ___."]

**Teacher:** "There's time. Learner 3, you haven't seen this game. Want to try?"

[As Learner 3: clicks a shelf, types 999, clicks Open. Read out what the game does. Then play on until Learner 3 escapes or gives up.]

**Teacher (to camera):** "The pair predicted that a new player would look at the tank after one wrong code. Here's what Learner 3 did: ___. That's the result we write down, whether or not it matches."

### Class minute 35-38: the end check [Paper] (Loom: about 20 seconds)

**Teacher:** "Same as before: on your own."

**Teacher (to camera):** "Learner 7 now does B and Learner 8 does A. Same scoring: reason, instruction, test, 0 to 2 each."

### Class minute 38-45: save, reopen, maker note, take home [Laptop + paper] (Loom: about 1.5 minutes)

**Teacher:** "Close your Lovable tab. Open your project again from imagi and check it's still there."

[Closes the Lovable tab. In imagi Edu, finds the project "Escape - pair 4" and opens it. Types 111. Expected: the fish tank wiggles, so this is Version 2.]

**Teacher (to camera):** "Saving and reopening on the student route isn't tested yet. If a project is missing, I note the pair number and tell Liba."

**Teacher:** "Maker note. A few words is enough. Then your take-home card. What would you add next?"

**Learner 7 (reading the maker note):** "I told AI to change the fish tank so it wiggles after a wrong code, and keep the code and the ending. The player kept clicking the door. We made the fish tank wiggle. The next test showed ___. Next I want to add a second clue on the hamster wheel. My first step would be to say where it goes."

**Teacher:** "What did you change that made it work better?"

**Learner 8:** "The wiggle. It shows you where to look, but you still have to count."

### After class: sharing, adult only [imagi/Lovable] (Loom: about 1 minute)

**Teacher (to camera):** "In class, the game was shared by playing it on our laptop. The take-home card has a drawing or a screenshot, no link. If Liba agrees to a link, I do this, not the child."

[In imagi, copies the project's own link. Opens a private window (Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome). Pastes it. Expected: a sign-in page.]

**Teacher (to camera):** "Before publishing, the imagi link asks for sign-in."

[Back in Lovable, clicks Publish. Opens a new private window and pastes the same imagi link. Expected: the game opens without sign-in and shows only the game. Plays one wrong code to check.]

**Teacher (to camera):** "After I publish from Lovable, the same link opens for anyone. So publishing is the adult's sharing decision, and an adult decides who gets the link. An unlisted link isn't private. Any play at home is recorded as untested."

### Close (Loom: about 30 seconds)

**Teacher (to camera):** "That's one pair's journey: their idea on paper, Version 1 in Lovable, a classmate played it, they chose a change from what the player did, the AI made it, and they retested it. Apart from the demo on the projector, everything happened on paper or in Lovable inside imagi. The prototype isn't part of this class. Nothing here has been tried with children yet. Next, I'd time this with a real class."
