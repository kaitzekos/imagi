# Student guide (projector slides)

Draft v0. One slide per section.

## 1. Can your classmates escape a world you invented?

[Play the example game for 20-30 seconds. Let the class shout guesses.]

## 2. Your idea first

Fill in the idea card with your partner.

- Your world: one place with one strange detail.
- Your clue: where the secret code hides.
- Your code: 3 numbers or one word.
- Your ending: what happens when someone escapes.

Each of you adds at least one line.

## 3. Build Version 1

Partner A is at the keyboard.

1. Copy the starting prompt.
2. Fill the four blanks with your idea, in your words.
3. Send it.

While it builds, each of you writes: "When I type the wrong code, I think the game will ______."

## 4. Try it and make it yours

Play your game. Was your prediction right?

Change one thing so it feels like your world: the clue, a message or the ending. Say it in your own words.

## 5. Swap and watch

Let the pair next to you play on your laptop.

Don't help. Don't point. Watch.

Write down one thing they did: where they clicked, where they got stuck, what made them laugh.

## 6. Version 2

Partner B is at the keyboard now.

Each of you suggests one change. Pick one. Say why.

Stuck? Use the help card: Change ______. Keep ______. Check ______.

## 7. Check it

Try your change. Did it do what you said?

Then try one thing that should still work, like Restart.

## 8. Save and next

Make sure your game is saved. Fill in your maker note.

What would you add next?
