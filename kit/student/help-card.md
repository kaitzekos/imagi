# Help card

Draft v0. Print one per pair.

## It's still building

Wait one more minute. Write your prediction. Still nothing after 3 minutes? Put your hand up.

## The game isn't what we wanted

Say what you saw, then what you want:

"I see ______. I want ______. Keep ______."

## Lovable says "Done"

Try it before you believe it. Did it really do what you asked?

## Our change made it worse

Go back to your last working version. [Steps added after testing.] Or put your hand up.

## We don't know what to change

- What did your player do just before they got stuck?
- What would make it fairer for a first-time player?
- What would make your player smile?

## Asking for a change

Change: ______ (what should be different)
Keep: ______ (what must still work)
Check: we will try ______ and expect ______.
